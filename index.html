This is a manual for a career that seems to have too many plot twists.

Every step I’ve taken might look like “variety” which is based on an underlying reason: Find the true meaning of good user experience. It is a challenge for small or traditional companies to have the time and capital to observe users. Through these experiences, I immersed myself in different teams, industries and culture to find which way could make a real impact rather than stick numerous notes on Figma. I’ve built the ability to dive into the effective workflow and what constraints and influence could shape the product, especially in a vague situation.

So now, I’ve turned those stories into my strengths. I’m looking for a long-term design role where I can grow with the team and contribute with focus and consistency. After all, a solution may sometimes be made from an irrelevant perspective.
